# Design Plan — Shree Balaji Pure Veg

Brief pins the palette: Dark Green + White + Gold + Orange, luxury/glassmorphism,
warm. Following it exactly rather than defaulting to cream/terracotta AI-cliché.

## Tokens
- --forest: #0F3D2E   (primary, deep pure-veg green — trust, tradition)
- --forest-deep: #082A20 (hero bg, nav bg)
- --ivory: #FBF7EF     (page background — warm white, not stark)
- --gold: #C9A227      (premium accent — borders, icons, prices)
- --ember: #D9642C     (CTA / action accent — orange-red, appetite-triggering)
- --charcoal: #241F18  (body text)
- --card: #FFFFFF

## Type
- Display: 'Fraunces' (serif, warm, has an Indian-thali roundness at high optical
  size, used sparingly for hero + section titles)
- Body/UI: 'Work Sans' (clean, legible, wide language support for Marathi/Hindi
  names in menu later if needed)

## Layout & Signature
Signature element: a hand-drawn gold "thali circle" divider — concentric rings
like a steel thali plate — used once at the top of the menu section instead of
a generic numbered marker, because a thali (full plate) is the actual cultural
object this restaurant is about, not decoration.

Hero: full-bleed deep-forest background with a subtle gold botanical-leaf
pattern (banana leaf / paan motif, common at pure-veg thali restaurants),
large Fraunces headline, four CTA pills.

Menu cards: white cards on ivory, gold hairline border on hover, veg-leaf
mark, category as an eyebrow label (not numbered — categories aren't a
sequence).

Motion: one orchestrated hero fade/rise on load; scroll-reveal on menu cards
(stagger); hover lift on cards/buttons. No decorative extras beyond that.
